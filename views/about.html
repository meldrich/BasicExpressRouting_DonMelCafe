About Don Mel Cafe

Don Mel Cafe started with one idea: a cup of coffee should take as long as it takes. We roast in small batches, grind to order, and never rush the pour.

Our beans come from growers in Batangas and Benguet. We buy directly where we can, and we pay for the harvest before it ripens so farmers are never left waiting.

The pastry case is filled each morning from our own oven. If it sells out, it sells out, and we bake again tomorrow.

Don Mel Cafe. Open daily, 7 AM to 9 PM.
